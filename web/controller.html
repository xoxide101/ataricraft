ToolsEmulatorStudio
Untested on hardware. These tools were built for the Atari Gamestation Gamepad (DGUNL-7152) before it was available. How it enumerates (HID gamepad, keyboard/mouse or custom) is unknown; use the diagnostics below to find out. The pad has a digital d-pad only (no analog stick).

3. Record the physical controls ("press this control")

Click Record, then press the button or move the control. Digital controls detect a button/key/HID bit; analog ones detect an axis, mouse movement, wheel or HID byte. Esc cancels. The result is saved in the profile's device map.

Fills joystick, console, aux, trackball, spinner and keypad from the recorded controls.
